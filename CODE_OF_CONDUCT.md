# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this project:

- being precise about facts, especially about which site or source a detail came from
- accepting that a site's markup changes and that a fix is a spec plus a fixture
- reporting a failure honestly instead of describing a partial result as complete
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- publishing someone's private conversation, screenshot, or account details
- deliberately misrepresenting a measurement or a verification state
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where someone
is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a report with the maintainer, or privately if the report
concerns the maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer will acknowledge a
report within three days and will explain what action follows, including why.

Maintainers who do not uphold this code in good faith may be removed from the project.
